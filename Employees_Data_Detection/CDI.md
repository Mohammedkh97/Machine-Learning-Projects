## 1️⃣ Programming & AI Development

| Term                                                           | Definition                                                            | Real-life Example                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Modifying Programs**                                         | Changing code to improve or fix it.                                   | Fixing a Python app that crashes when you enter a wrong password.         |
| **Impact of Changes**                                          | The effects of changing a program (good or bad).                      | Changing a loop that accidentally causes a game to freeze.                |
| **Training**                                                   | Teaching an AI model by feeding it lots of data.                      | Training a model with pictures of cats and dogs so it can recognize them. |

## Answers:
---

### **E-CDI-IC.25**

**How does effective cross-referencing contribute to identifying and mitigating bias in AI-generated outputs?**
→ It helps by comparing AI responses with reliable sources to detect incorrect or biased information, reducing the spread of bias.

**What distinguishes Copilot from other AI chatbots?**
→ Copilot integrates directly into software tools (like Microsoft Word or GitHub) to assist with tasks, while other chatbots focus on conversation.

**What is AI and what is not?**
→ AI is software that can perform tasks that usually need human intelligence, like language translation. A simple calculator is not AI because it follows fixed instructions.

**Why is it important to tailor AI prompts for different departments?**
→ Each department (HR, marketing, IT) needs different types of answers — tailoring prompts ensures the AI gives relevant, useful responses.

---

### **E-CDI-IC.26**

**The role of data in powering AI**
→ Data is what AI learns from; good data helps AI make accurate predictions.

---

### **E-CDI-IC.27**

**How have AI chatbots influenced different industries?**
→ AI chatbots handle customer service in retail, assist patients in healthcare, and provide legal info in law firms.

**How can AI improve video creation processes?**
→ AI can automate editing, add subtitles, or suggest improvements in videos.

**How can AI prompts optimize sales performance?**
→ AI can analyze customer data and suggest the best ways to approach leads.

**How does AI transform marketing and social media management?**
→ AI creates social media posts, analyzes trends, and improves targeting for ads.

**How is generative AI transforming art and design?**
→ It helps artists by generating ideas, patterns, and drafts quickly.

**In what ways can AI support HR operations?**
→ AI can screen job applications, schedule interviews, and answer employee questions.

**In what ways is AI impacting music production?**
→ AI can compose music, suggest improvements, or mix tracks.

**Narrow AI vs. General AI**
→ Narrow AI performs one specific task (like language translation). General AI would perform any human task (but it doesn't exist yet).

**Real-life examples of Narrow AI**
→ Siri, Google Translate, or spam filters in email.

**What is the significance of DALL-E in creative AI applications?**
→ DALL-E generates images from text descriptions, helping artists visualize ideas.

**What makes Suno.ai valuable for musicians and creators?**
→ Suno.ai helps musicians create new music or beats using AI.

**What role does Runway ML play in AI-based video production?**
→ Runway ML provides AI tools to edit videos, add effects, and create content.

**Why are AI prompts valuable for product development teams?**
→ They help teams quickly explore ideas and improve product design.

**Why do we study AI?**
→ To understand how it works, how it affects society, and how to use it ethically.

**Why might artists choose Midjourney for their projects?**
→ Midjourney generates artistic images based on text, helping artists explore new styles.

---

### **E-CDI-IC.28**

**Can AI become General: The Turing Test**
→ The Turing Test checks if an AI can mimic human conversation so well that people can't tell the difference — passing this is a step toward General AI.

**Why must data privacy and security be prioritized in AI applications?**
→ AI uses personal data, so protecting it prevents misuse and builds trust.

### **E-CDI.AP.22**

**Best Prompt = Role + Specificity + Structure + Constraints**
→ Example: “As a nutritionist, write a 300-word article about healthy diets for teenagers, using clear language.”

**Lifecycle of AI: Training and inference explained**
→ Training: AI learns from data. Inference: AI uses what it learned to make predictions.

**What is the 'Rule of Thumb' in prompt engineering?**
→ Be clear and specific in your prompt to get better AI responses.

### **E-CDI.AP.23**

**How do examples of effective prompts enhance learning?**
→ They show how to ask better questions, leading to clearer answers from AI.

**How does AI improve document summarization?**
→ AI can quickly shorten long documents into key points.

**What causes AI hallucinations?**
→ AI may make things up when it lacks correct data or the prompt is unclear.

**What is the importance of mastering prompt engineering?**
→ It helps you get more useful, accurate, and reliable AI outputs.

**What role does AI play in software programming?**
→ AI can suggest code, find bugs, or help write documentation.

**What skills are developed through prompt engineering practice?**
→ Clear thinking, communication, and understanding how AI works.

**What skills are essential for creating AI-generated content?**
→ Creativity, clear writing, and knowing how to guide the AI.

**Why is clarity essential when crafting prompts?**
→ Clear prompts prevent confusion and reduce AI errors.

**Why is iteration important in prompt engineering?**
→ Trying different versions of prompts improves the results.

### **E-CDI.AP.24**

**Relationship between AI and Machine Learning**
→ Machine Learning is a part of AI — it’s the method by which AI learns from data.
