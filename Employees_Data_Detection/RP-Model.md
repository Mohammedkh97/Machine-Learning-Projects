### **Section II – Written Question**

**Q:** In what ways might the ongoing advancements in Artificial Intelligence exacerbate the existing digital divide, and what measures could be taken to mitigate these potential negative impacts?

**Answer:**
AI advancements could widen the digital divide because people in underserved communities may lack access to AI tools, training, or infrastructure (internet, devices). This can lead to fewer opportunities and increase inequality.
➡️ *For example: if AI-powered education apps are only available to wealthy schools, students in poorer areas fall further behind.*

**Mitigation measures:**
✅ Provide affordable internet and devices.
✅ Offer free or low-cost digital literacy and AI education programs.
✅ Develop AI tools that work on low-cost hardware.
✅ Encourage government policies that promote tech access for all.
